# Lease Accountant Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 15, 2026 · Version 2026-10-15

## 1. Who We Are

MS Finance Group ("we," "us," or "our") operates Lease Accountant, a product associated with www.ms-consult.us. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle personal information when businesses and their Users use the Service. For Customer Data, Customer generally determines the purposes for which personal information is processed, and we process that information on Customer's behalf. Where the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) applies, Customer may be a "business" and we may act as its "service provider," as those terms are defined by applicable law. For our own account, billing, security, support, and website data, we determine the purposes of processing.

## 2. Data We Collect

| Category | Examples | Source |
 --- | --- | --- |
 Account data | Name, work email, company name, role, hashed password, MFA settings | You, when you sign up or are invited |
 Customer Data | Lease contracts, lessor names and contacts, payments, schedules, journal entries, branches, uploaded files | You and your Users |
 Billing data | Billing name and address, plan, invoices, last 4 digits and card expiry | Stripe; we do not receive full card numbers |
 Usage and log data | IP address, browser, pages used, timestamps, audit log of changes, error logs | Collected automatically |
 Security data | Bot-protection check results at sign-in and sign-up | Cloudflare Turnstile |
 Browser storage | Sign-in session token and application preferences | Set by the app |

We do not use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies. Local browser storage is used to maintain sign-in sessions and remember application settings. Clearing it may sign you out or reset local preferences.

## 3. How We Use Data

- To provide the Service, including calculating lease schedules, recording entries, and producing reports.
- To manage accounts, trials, subscriptions, billing, and invoices.
- To secure the Service through MFA, bot protection, rate limits, audit trails, access controls, and fraud prevention.
- To diagnose errors and monitor uptime, security, and performance.
- To send service communications, such as authentication messages, billing notices, support responses, and notices of material changes.
- To send marketing emails only where permitted and with any consent required by applicable law; recipients may opt out of marketing communications.
- To comply with legal, tax, accounting, and regulatory obligations.

We do not sell personal information or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

## 4. Where the Service Is Offered

The Service is offered only to businesses located in the United States. We do not market the Service to, or knowingly serve, customers in the European Economic Area, United Kingdom, or Switzerland.

## 8. Security

We use administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards designed to protect personal information and Customer Data. These safeguards currently include:

- database-level separation of Organization data using row-level security;
- encryption in transit using HTTPS/TLS and encryption at rest provided by our hosting infrastructure;
- optional multi-factor authentication;
- automatic sign-out after 30 minutes of inactivity;
- bot protection and rate limiting;
- audit trails and error monitoring;
- daily backups; and
- limited production-data access for authorized personnel who need access to operate or support the Service.

No information system is completely secure. If a security incident involving personal information requires notification under applicable law, we will provide notification as required by law.

California residents may have additional rights where the CCPA applies, including rights to know, access, correct, or delete certain personal information and the right not to receive discriminatory treatment for exercising applicable privacy rights.
